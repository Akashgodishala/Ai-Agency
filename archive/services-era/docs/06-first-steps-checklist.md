# 06 — First Steps (do this week)

> Forget the big picture for a moment. Here is exactly what to do **first**. Small,
> concrete, doable. You don't need money, code, or a team to start these.

---

## Step 1 — Answer 4 questions (30 minutes)

Write down your answers. These four decisions unlock everything else:

1. **Niche:** Which type of business will you serve first? (Pick one. Whoever you
   can most easily reach — an industry you know or have contacts in — wins.)
2. **Agent:** Which agent will you sell first? (Recommend: **Lead Response** or
   **Customer Support** — easiest to build, easiest ROI to prove.)
3. **Outcome:** Finish this sentence — *"I help [niche] achieve [result] without
   [pain]."*
4. **Budget:** How much can you spend on tools per month while landing client #1?
   (Even $50–$100 is enough to start.)

> Don't have clear answers? That's fine — tell me what you *do* know (your
> background, who you know, what interests you) and I'll help you choose.

---

## Step 2 — Let me set up your foundation (I do this)

Once you've answered Step 1, I will build, right here in this repo:

- [ ] A **client intake form** (the questions from Station 1).
- [ ] An **Agent Spec template** (what you fill in before building each agent).
- [ ] A **project tracker** (to manage clients through the 6 stations).
- [ ] A **test checklist** for your chosen agent type.
- [ ] A **simple landing page** describing your offer.
- [ ] Draft **outreach messages** for your first prospects.

You just answer Step 1; I produce the rest.

---

## Step 3 — Build your first demo agent (together)

- [ ] Pick a realistic imaginary client in your niche.
- [ ] We design its Agent Spec (5 ingredients).
- [ ] I help you build it in a no-code tool (or I build custom parts).
- [ ] We test it against the checklist.
- [ ] You record a short demo video.

**This demo is your #1 sales asset.** Everything after this gets easier.

---

## Step 4 — Reach out to 10 businesses (you do this)

- [ ] List 10 real businesses in your niche.
- [ ] Send each a personalized message offering a free tailored demo. (I draft
      these; you send and add a personal touch.)
- [ ] Book discovery calls with anyone interested.

---

## Step 5 — Deliver your first pilot

- [ ] Run the interested client through the 6-station delivery system.
- [ ] Charge a **founding-client price** in exchange for a testimonial.
- [ ] Deliver, support, and collect the testimonial.

🎉 **That's your first paying client.** Repeat, systematize, raise prices.

## A note on expectations (honest talk)

- This is a **real business**, not a magic button. The plan is proven, but it
  needs your consistent effort on the parts only a human can do: talking to
  clients, building relationships, and making judgment calls.
- The **technology is the easy part** now — I handle most of it. The hard,
  valuable part is **sales and trust**. That's your job, and it's learnable.
- Start **small and specific**, get real wins, then scale. "World's top" is the
  destination; "first happy client in your niche" is the next step.

You've got a solid plan and a partner who can build. Let's get your first client.

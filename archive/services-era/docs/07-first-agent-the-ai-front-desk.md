# 07 — Your First Agent: the "AI Front Desk"

> Decision recorded: this is the **first agent you build, demo, and sell**, based
> on your chosen niche (local services & clinics) and services-first approach.

## Why this one

For local businesses and clinics, the deepest, most provable pain is **missed
calls and after-hours inquiries turning into lost customers.** The AI Front Desk
attacks that directly and bundles the three highest-value agent types from the
[catalog](03-agent-catalog.md) into one clean offer:

- **Customer Support** — answers common questions instantly.
- **Lead Response & Qualification** — greets and captures every new inquiry.
- **Appointment / Scheduling** — books, reschedules, sends reminders.

One agent, one story: **"Never lose another patient because no one answered."**

## Why it's the right *first* product

- **Easiest ROI to prove** — "you missed X calls; each is worth $Y" is a number
  the owner feels immediately (your landing page calculator shows it live).
- **Buildable fast** — mostly no-code; live in about a week.
- **Repeatable** — nearly every clinic and local shop needs the same thing, so
  each build gets faster and becomes a reusable template.
- **Expandable** — once they trust it, you upsell voice, multi-location, etc.

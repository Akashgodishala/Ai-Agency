# 03 — Agent Catalog (the menu you sell)

> Instead of "we build any agent for any use case," you sell from a **menu of
> proven agent types**. Each is a repeatable product with a known build recipe.
> ~90% of real client requests map to one of these. Custom work is the exception,
> priced higher.

For each agent below: what it does, who buys it, what it needs, and roughly how
hard it is to build (🟢 easy / 🟡 medium / 🔴 advanced).

---

## 1. 🟢 Customer Support Agent

**Does:** Answers customer questions 24/7 on the website chat, WhatsApp, or
email. Escalates to a human when needed.

**Who buys:** E-commerce, SaaS, clinics, local services — anyone drowning in
repetitive questions.

**Needs:** Their FAQ/policies/product info (knowledge), a human-handoff rule, and
optionally order/account lookup.

**Sells as:** "Cut support workload by 70% and never leave a customer waiting."

---

## 2. 🟢 Lead Response & Qualification Agent

**Does:** Instantly replies to new leads (form, DM, missed call), asks
qualifying questions, and books the good ones onto a calendar.

**Who buys:** Real estate, agencies, coaches, high-ticket local services,
B2B sales teams.

**Needs:** Qualifying questions, calendar integration, CRM to log leads.

**Sells as:** "Every lead answered in 60 seconds — stop losing deals to slow
follow-up." (Often the easiest ROI to prove → great first offer.)

---

## 3. 🟡 Internal Knowledge Assistant

**Does:** Staff ask it questions and it answers from the company's own documents,
policies, SOPs, and data.

**Who buys:** Any company with 10+ staff, lots of documents, and repeated
"where's the doc / what's the policy" questions.

**Needs:** Their internal documents (securely), access control.

**Sells as:** "Your team's questions answered instantly from your own knowledge —
no more digging through drives."

---

## 4. 🟡 Content / Marketing Agent

**Does:** Turns one input (a call, an article, a product) into many outputs —
social posts, emails, blog drafts, product descriptions — in the brand voice.

**Who buys:** Marketing teams, course creators, agencies, e-commerce.

**Needs:** Brand voice examples, content guidelines, publishing destinations.

**Sells as:** "A month of on-brand content from a single briefing."

---

## 5. 🟡 Data Extraction & Processing Agent

**Does:** Reads documents (invoices, resumes, contracts, forms, emails) and
pulls out structured data into a spreadsheet or system.

**Who buys:** Accounting, recruiting, legal, ops-heavy businesses.

**Needs:** Sample documents, the target fields, where the data goes.

**Sells as:** "Turn stacks of documents into clean data automatically."

---

## 6. 🟡 Appointment / Scheduling Agent

**Does:** Handles booking, rescheduling, reminders, and no-show follow-ups over
chat, SMS, or voice.

**Who buys:** Clinics, salons, consultants, home services.

**Needs:** Calendar/booking system, business hours, reminder rules.

**Sells as:** "Fill your calendar and cut no-shows — automatically."

---

## 7. 🔴 Outbound Outreach / SDR Agent

**Does:** Researches prospects, personalizes and sends outreach, handles replies,
and books interested leads. (Handle carefully — must respect anti-spam laws and
platform rules.)

**Who buys:** B2B sales teams, agencies, recruiters.

**Needs:** Target list, offer, email/LinkedIn sending infra, compliance rules.

**Sells as:** "A tireless SDR that fills your pipeline." *(Advanced; do this once
you've delivered simpler agents and understand compliance.)*

---

## 8. 🔴 Voice Agent (phone)

**Does:** Answers or makes phone calls — booking, FAQ, reminders — in a natural
voice.

**Who buys:** Clinics, home services, high call-volume local businesses.

**Needs:** Phone integration, scripts, escalation to humans.

**Sells as:** "Never miss a call again." *(High wow-factor, more moving parts —
tackle after you're comfortable.)*

---

## How to use this catalog

1. **Start by selling ONE agent type** (recommend #1 Support or #2 Lead
   Response — easiest to build, easiest ROI to prove).
2. Build **one great demo** of it for your niche. That demo is your #1 sales
   asset.
3. Add more menu items only after you've delivered the first a few times.
4. Anything a client wants that's not on the menu = "custom," quoted higher, and
   if it repeats, it becomes a new menu item.

---

## Build-difficulty reality check (for a non-technical founder)

- 🟢 items: buildable **this week** with no-code tools + my help.
- 🟡 items: buildable in **1–2 weeks**, may need connecting a couple of systems.
- 🔴 items: buildable, but do them **after** you have wins and some revenue —
  they have compliance and reliability considerations.

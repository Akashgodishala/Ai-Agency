# Outreach Kit — messages that book demos

> Ready-to-use scripts for landing your first clinic/local-service clients.
> Rules that make these work:
> 1. **Lead with their pain and a specific observation**, not "we do AI."
> 2. **Offer a free, tailored demo** — low friction, high curiosity.
> 3. **One clear call to action** per message.
> 4. **Personalize the first line** every time (mention their business by name /
>    something real you noticed). Generic = ignored.
> 5. Keep it short. Send from a real name, not "the team."
>
> Replace `[[ ]]` placeholders. Tools like the target list help you track who got what.

---

## How to find targets (15 minutes)
- Google Maps your niche + city (e.g. "dental clinic Austin"). Look for:
  - Good reviews but **no online booking** on their site (they're missing leads).
  - Small/independent (owner makes decisions fast).
  - A visible phone number and website.
- Log 20–30 into `target-list-template.csv`.

## Numbers to expect (so you don't get discouraged)
- Cold email: ~1–5% reply, a fraction book a call. **Volume matters** — send 20–30, not 3.
- Warm intros / DMs to people you know convert far higher. **Start there if you can.**
- The **demo link does the selling.** Make sure every message includes it.
- Goal for month one: **enough conversations to land 1–3 founding clients.**
